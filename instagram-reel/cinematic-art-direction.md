# Cinematic Reel

Uses the built-in image generation tool for an original photorealistic scene, then a local Python renderer for camera motion, speaker cues, captions, and the existing synthetic dialogue.

## Image prompt

A vertical cinematic movie still of an original thoughtful young adult woman with medium brown skin, dark shoulder-length hair, and a tailored dark jacket, debating an original brushed-titanium humanoid robot with subtle cyan eyes. Seated across a polished table in a futuristic observatory lounge, rain-streaked windows and a distant city at night. Warm amber light on the human and teal practical lighting, realistic skin and mechanical textures, shallow depth of field. Both heads and upper torsos visible in the middle 60% of the portrait. Dark space at top and bottom for titles and subtitles. No text, logos, or watermarks. No resemblance to existing actors or movie characters.

## Deliverables

- `cinematic-scene.png`: generated scene artwork.
- `human-vs-ai-cinematic-hi-aidelulu.mp4`: 1080 × 1920 movie-style Reel.
- `cinematic-cover.png`: cover image.
- `create_cinematic_reel.py`: local renderer.

This version uses a generated still with animated camera crops. It does not have generated facial acting or lip-sync. It retains macOS Samantha and Daniel synthetic voices. The original cartoon version is preserved.
